# Open GRIR Dashboard

## Overview

The Open GRIR Dashboard is a Power BI solution designed to monitor and analyze open Goods Receipt / Invoice Receipt (GRIR) balances across multiple business dimensions.

The dashboard enables finance and procurement teams to identify outstanding GRIR amounts, track trends, and analyze balances by Desk, GL Description, Business Area, Plant, Fiscal Year, Quarter, and Month.

---

## Features

### Dashboard 1: Desk Wise Open GRIR Analysis

- View total open GRIR amount.
- Analyze GRIR balances by business desk.
- Quarter-wise GRIR comparison.
- Interactive filters:
- Business Area
- Plant Name
- Year
- Quarter
- Month
- Visualizations:
- Summary KPI Card
- Desk-wise Matrix Table
- Bar Chart Analysis
- Pie Chart Distribution

### Dashboard 2: GL Wise Open GRIR Analysis

- Track open GRIR balances by GL Description.
- Quarter-wise GL balance visibility.
- Analyze contribution by GL Category.
- Interactive slicing and filtering capabilities.
- Visualizations:
- KPI Card
- GL Description Matrix
- Bar Chart
- Category-wise Pie Chart

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Total GRIR Amount | Total outstanding GRIR amount |
| Q1 Amount | Quarter 1 Outstanding Amount |
| Q2 Amount | Quarter 2 Outstanding Amount |
| Q3 Amount | Quarter 3 Outstanding Amount |
| Q4 Amount | Quarter 4 Outstanding Amount |
| Desk Wise Total | Outstanding amount by Desk |
| GL Wise Total | Outstanding amount by GL Description |

---

## Dashboard Filters

The report supports dynamic filtering through:

- Business Area
- Plant Name
- Fiscal Year
- Quarter
- Month

These filters allow users to drill down into specific business segments and periods.

---

## Business Benefits

- Improves visibility of pending GRIR balances.
- Supports month-end and year-end reconciliation.
- Identifies high-value outstanding transactions.
- Enables proactive action on unresolved GRIR items.
- Helps finance teams monitor procurement and invoice matching performance.
